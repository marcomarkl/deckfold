Thirteenth pre-release of deckfold: corrections to the presenter view of v0.10.0 after it was used with a real deck. The panes keep their names, the buttons at the top show icons, the top bar has no counters any more, and the strip of slides can be made larger.

## What changed

- **"Live" and "Preview".** The large pane on the left is now called "Live", the pane on the right "Preview". A pane and its switch in the status bar have the same name. In v0.10.0 the right pane was called "Next step" or "Next slide", depending on what the next click did, while its switch read "Next click", and it was framed in blue before a change of slide. It shows the same as before, the state the next click leads to, but its name and colour no longer change. The arrow after the dots still turns blue when the next click changes the slide. Decision E20 is updated.
- **A− and A+ are off while the notes are hidden.** They change only the size of the notes.
- **Icons instead of words.** In the presenter view, search, full screen and end show are buttons with an icon: a magnifier, four corners (pointing out in a window, pointing in in full screen) and the on/off sign, still in red. Their names show as a tooltip. The search on the start screen is the same magnifier, next to the keyboard icon.
- **No counters in the top bar.** The slide counter ("3 / 9") and the step counter are gone; the strip of slides marks the slide, and the dots show the steps. The status bar shows the number of slides at its left. On the start screen a deck with one slide now reads "1 slide" instead of "1 slides".
- **A larger strip of slides.** The divider above the strip of slides in the presenter view can be dragged up, and the tiles grow with it, up to almost half the height of the window. The width of a tile is stored with the other settings, and in a configuration file as `presenter.layout.strip` (96 to 480 pixels). `--refresh` gives files of earlier versions the default of 132.
- **The switch of the presenter view after the end of a show.** When a show ended, the start screen showed the switch of the presenter view off, and it turned on up to a second later, when the presenter view sent its next sign of life. The start screen now knows from before the reload that the presenter view is open and shows the switch on at once. If no presenter view reports within three seconds, the switch goes off.

## What is tested

- Browser tests for each change: the names of the panes and switches before the start, during the show and at the end, and no frame on "Preview"; A− and A+ off while the notes are hidden; the icons and their names; no counter in the top bar and the number of slides in the status bar; dragging the strip of slides, the stored width after a reload and the limit at 45 % of the window height; the switch of the presenter view right after the end of a show, without a presenter view after a reload, and switched off before the presenter view reports. The ten new or changed tests fail on the code of v0.10.0.
- Go tests for `presenter.layout.strip`: the default, a value read from a configuration, values outside 96 to 480 refused, and the default added by `--refresh` to a file without it.
- The presenter view at 1280 x 800 in the middle and at the last step of a slide with steps, with the notes hidden, with a larger strip of slides, and at 700 pixels wide; the start screen at 1280 x 800.
- In Chrome and WebKit on a Mac: the 162 browser tests. The tests of the switch and the strip passed 5 repeats each in Chrome.
- The six binaries build with `scripts/release.sh` on a Mac (19.3 MB to 21.2 MB), and the smoke test passes with the one for macOS (arm64).
- The CI: `go vet`, the Go suite and the smoke test on Linux, Windows and macOS (x64 and arm64), and the browser tests with the public decks in Chrome on Linux and Windows and in Firefox on Linux.
