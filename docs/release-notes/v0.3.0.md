Third pre-release of deckfold: the known gaps of the PowerPoint converter are closed.

v0.2.0 listed what deckfold could not show yet: embedded fonts, triggers, 3D, Morph and the dynamic transitions, several chart types, WMF pictures, right-to-left text and a few text features. This release draws all of them. Some of these features did not occur in any real deck tested so far; they are checked with generated feature decks and reference images from PowerPoint.

## What is new

- Fonts embedded in a presentation are read, also when PowerPoint stored them with MicroType Express compression. deckfold has its own decompressor for the format.
- Animated GIFs start again when their slide is shown.
- Triggers: a click on a shape plays the effects that shape starts. A click on a video or an audio icon plays or pauses it.
- 3D shapes (bevels, extrusions, contours, materials, cameras and lights) are drawn with WebGL. deckfold embeds three.js only in decks that need it. Without WebGL, and in the previews of the presenter view, an SVG approximation takes their place.
- Picture bullets, inner shadows, soft edges, reflections, blur and effect containers.
- WMF pictures.
- Justified and distributed text. Right-to-left text and complex scripts (Arabic, Hebrew, Indic, Thai) are measured with HarfBuzz (go-text/typesetting), and their fonts are embedded whole so that the browser can shape them.
- Animations by word and by letter.
- Morph pairs objects by their "!!" names, otherwise by kind and text, and moves, scales, turns and recolors them.
- The PowerPoint 2010 and later transitions: cube, flip, gallery, doors, window, ferris wheel, conveyor, pan, fly through, page curl and others with CSS 3D; glitter, honeycomb, vortex, shred, ripple, fracture, crush, curtains, wind, prestige, airplane and origami with WebGL, as a fade without it.
- Charts: scatter, bubble, radar, stock, pie of pie, bar of pie and surface charts, and 3D column, bar, line, area and pie charts.
- Ink.

Converting uses less memory again: deckfold now writes the output to the file as it goes. For the largest deck of the test collection, the peak fell from about 1.7 GB to about 850 MB, and the output stays byte for byte the same.

## What is tested

- Twelve new public feature decks in `fixtures/pptx`, one per group of features, each opened and saved by PowerPoint 16.114 and compared with PowerPoint's PDF export by SSIM. The new decks reach 0.953 to 1.000, except the charts of the new chart types (0.74 to 0.90, because PowerPoint sets their text in another font and lays out the plot area itself) and the deck with an embedded font (0.92, because the first baseline of that font sits a few pixels lower in PowerPoint).
- Two decks whose animations and transitions PowerPoint added itself through AppleScript, so that the tests read the XML as PowerPoint writes it.
- Browser tests for GIFs, triggers, 3D shapes, word and letter builds, Morph, the 3D transitions and the 3D charts. They run in Chrome on macOS, Linux and Windows and in Firefox on Linux; a few PowerPoint cases also run in WebKit.
- A test that a picture fill inherited from a layout placeholder is shown.
- 46 PowerPoint files from a private collection: 42 convert, and 4 contain videos larger than 1 GB, which deckfold refuses with a hint. Their embedded fonts load in Chrome, and all of their 3D shapes are drawn.

## Not tested yet

- Keynote exports in the CI: Keynote 15.4 does not open files through AppleScript in the test folder, and its exports contain Apple's player, so they stay local.
- 3D shapes in a browser with a GPU: the tests draw WebGL with SwiftShader.

## Known limits

- Charts with an automatic layout can differ from PowerPoint by a few pixels, and the 3D charts are an approximation.
- 3D shapes and the WebGL transitions are close to PowerPoint, not identical; Morph does not change the outline of shapes.
- Animations started by a bookmark in a video or audio clip are not played.
- Videos in WMV, AVI or ASF show only their still image. Media files larger than 1 GB are refused.
