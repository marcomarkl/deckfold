Sixth pre-release of deckfold: the file now knows what is written on its slides.

## Why

Until now deckfold knew how a deck runs: how many slides and steps it has, their timings and the notes. What is on a slide it knew only for PowerPoint decks, and only while it drew them. A talk with forty slides could be moved through by number or by looking for the right thumbnail, and nothing outside the browser could read the text of a converted deck.

## What changed

- **Search and outline.** "Search" on the start screen and in the presenter view (or `/`, Ctrl+F, Cmd+F) opens a list of all slides with their titles. Typing searches the slide text and leaves the slides that hold it, each with the first place found. On the start screen the choice becomes the start slide; in the presenter view it goes to the slide at once, like a click in the strip of slides. The presenter view searches the notes too. The start screen does not, because it may already be on the projector, and the presentation window has no search at all.
- **Titles only where the source marks them.** A PowerPoint slide has its title placeholder, a claude.ai slide its label. Keynote exports mark no title, so those slides are listed by their number and the start of their text. deckfold does not guess a title from the size or the place of a text.
- **Links to other slides** work in PowerPoint decks and in Keynote exports: on text and on shapes, to a slide, to the next, previous, first or last slide, to the slide viewed before, and to the end of the show. Both windows follow. Before, PowerPoint drew such links as text without an action, and a click on one in a Keynote export moved on like any click. A link to a hidden or skipped slide is no link and is named in the report.
- **`--content` writes the slide text for other programs.** `deckfold deck.html --content deck.json` writes every slide with its title, its notes and its text blocks: the role of each (title, subtitle, text, list entry with its level, picture description) and the step in which it shows. `--content deck.md` writes the same as Markdown, without the steps. `docs/content-model.md` describes the fields. Text from the slides is escaped in the Markdown.
- **`--refresh` adds the slide text to older files** from claude.ai and Keynote: these files hold everything it is read from. A PowerPoint file from before has to be converted again from the presentation; `--refresh` says so and still updates the controls.
- **No slide number display of the Keynote player in the presentation.** The player showed it for a moment whenever deckfold jumped to a slide; it was already hidden in the previews.
- The configuration has a new key action `find` (default `/`).

The slides themselves are untouched: the players of claude.ai and Keynote run as before, and the source HTML is not changed for any of this.

## What Keynote exports do not give

- The text of a Keynote slide comes from what the export lists for screen readers: in the order of that list, which is not always top to bottom, with the name of a picture instead of its description and without the text of tables.
- The export does not say in which step a text shows, so the blocks of a Keynote deck have no step.
- A Keynote file converted before this version follows its links only after it is converted again; `--refresh` adds its slide text, not its links.

## What is tested

- The content model of a generated PowerPoint deck against what the generator put on the slides: roles, list levels, the step of every block, links, and what must not be in it (footer, slide number, the hidden slide). In the browser, every block shows in the player from its step on and not before, for the PowerPoint deck and for a claude.ai deck with builds.
- Search and outline in Chrome and WebKit: opening by button and by key, filtering, notes only in the presenter view, the keys staying with the overlay while it is open, the choice going to the slide, and slide text with markup staying text.
- Links to other slides: every kind of target, on text and on a shape, with the presentation, the player and the presenter view on the same slide afterwards; for Keynote with a local export of the content deck.
- `--content` as JSON and Markdown, and `--refresh` on files without the slide text, for all three formats, also with damaged slide data in the file.
