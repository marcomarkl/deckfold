Second pre-release of deckfold: PowerPoint files are converted directly.

deckfold now reads `.pptx`, `.ppsx` and `.pptm` files without a detour through Keynote. It draws every slide as SVG, lays out the text itself, embeds subsets of the fonts and plays animations and transitions with its own player. The control layer is the same as for Keynote and claude.ai decks: start screen, presenter view with previews, notes and overview, auto mode, screen choice and clicker control. PowerPoint decks bring their presenter notes along. Google Slides decks work the same way through File > Download > Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx).

## What is converted

- Shapes with all preset geometries of ECMA-376 and custom geometry, fills (solid, gradient, pattern, picture), outlines with dashes and arrowheads, shadows and glow, rotation, flips and groups
- Text with the inheritance from layouts and masters, bullets and numbering, alignment, line spacing, shrink on overflow, vertical text and slide numbers
- Pictures (also SVG and EMF), tables, charts (column, bar, line, area, pie, doughnut), SmartArt graphics, the fallback pictures of OLE objects and equations, audio and video
- Animations: entrance, exit and emphasis effects, motion paths, builds by paragraph, effects with and after the previous one; transitions fade, push, wipe, cover, reveal, split, circle, zoom and cut; automatic timings for auto mode; hidden slides are left out

Converting uses less memory than before, for all formats: a presentation of 363 MB needs about 1.4 GB instead of 3.2 GB, and the output stays byte for byte the same.

The report lists the fonts and where they came from (the presentation, the computer, or the fonts bundled with deckfold) and everything deckfold could not show as PowerPoint does.

## What is tested

- Go unit tests for every part of the converter, structural tests of the public feature decks in `fixtures/pptx`, and a test that markup, script links and SVG scripts from a hostile deck never reach the output in executable form.
- Browser tests in Chrome on macOS and, in the CI job, on Linux: navigation, presenter view with previews and notes, overview, `--refresh`, media, every animation step reached by jumping and by playing, previews without animation, auto mode with the timings of the file, and a deck with 200 slides (conversion, presenter view and steps well under a second).
- Comparison with PowerPoint: every feature deck was opened and saved by PowerPoint 16.114 and exported as PDF; each slide of deckfold's output is compared with it by SSIM. All slides reach 0.966 to 0.999, except two chart slides (0.68 and 0.92) whose gridlines are offset by a few pixels.
- 41 PowerPoint files from a private collection: 37 convert. One is no PowerPoint file, and three contain videos larger than 1 GB, which deckfold refuses with a hint.
- Table styles: the text color and weight of a table style (for example the white bold header row) take priority over the presentation's default text styles; a test with a generated deck covers it.

## Not tested yet

- The image comparison in Chromium on Linux: the reference images come from PowerPoint on macOS, and the CI job compares them on Linux for the first time.
- PowerPoint decks in WebKit (Safari) and on Windows.

## Known limits

- Fonts embedded in a presentation are stored with MicroType Express compression, which deckfold cannot read yet. It uses the installed font or a bundled substitute and reports it.
- Morph and the 3D transitions are played as a fade; animations by word or letter are played by paragraph; triggers are not played.
- WMF pictures and scatter, radar, bubble and stock charts are not converted; 3D effects are left out.
- Videos in WMV, AVI or ASF show only their still image.
- Right-to-left text and complex scripts (Arabic, Indic) are laid out without shaping; picture bullets are not shown.
